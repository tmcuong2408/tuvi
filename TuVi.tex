\begin{abstract}
	Since ancient times, traditional destiny analysis has been regarded as an Eastern predictive discipline, applying astronomical and calendrical laws to decode human life trajectories through horoscopic charts. However, stripping away its mystical veil and examining it through the lens of modern mathematics reveals that this system is fundamentally a remarkably rigorous information encoding model.

	This monograph formalizes and systematizes the field of destiny analysis from a mathematical perspective, defining it under a novel designation: {Formal Destiny Dynamics}.

	Within the mathematical space of Formal Destiny Dynamics, an individual chart is not a mystical fate, but rather a universal universe composed of partitioned subsets (the 12-palace domain system) and interacting elements (the set of celestial stars). By employing the language of Set Theory—such as mappings from birth times into chart space, intersections representing structural configurations (patterns), unions connecting linked domains, or functions describing the temporal shifts of life phases—Formal Destiny Dynamics strips away religious ambiguity to restore its core essence: an ancient spatial-temporal data analysis algorithm, digitized and structured under pure logical reasoning.
\end{abstract}

\section{Axioms of Destiny Dynamics}

Let $\Omega$ be the universal set of all objects in the universe. We establish the following axiomatic system:
\begin{enumerate}
	\item \textbf{Axiom of Classification.} For every element $\omega \in \Omega$, either $\omega \in \Omega_+$ or $\omega \in \Omega_-$, and there exists a mapping $\Phi : \Omega \to \{-1,1\}$ satisfying:
	      $$\Phi(\omega) := \begin{cases}
			      1 & \text{if } \omega \in \Omega_+  \\
			      -1 & \text{if } \omega \in \Omega_- \\
		      \end{cases}
	      $$
	\item \textbf{Axiom of Equilibrium:} \[
		      \sum_{\omega \in \Omega}^{}{\Phi(\omega)} = 0
	      \]
	\item \textbf{Axiom of Completeness:} \[\Omega = \Omega_- \cup \Omega_+\]
	\item \textbf{Axiom of Separation:} \[\Omega_- \cap \Omega_+ = \emptyset\]

\end{enumerate}
where
\begin{align*}
	 & \Omega_+:= \{\omega \in \Omega : \omega \text{ possesses Yang polarity}\} \\
	 & \Omega_-:= \{\omega \in \Omega : \omega \text{ possesses Yin polarity}\}    \\
\end{align*}

\section{Core Concepts}
\begin{definition}[Destiny Resolution]
	Given a positive integer $n$ such that $\log_2(n)$ is an integer, the universal set $\Omega$ can be partitioned into $2^n$ disjoint subsets. We call $n$ the \textbf{destiny resolution}.
\end{definition}
\begin{remark}
	Throughout this document, we assume that the destiny resolution $n$ is an even positive integer.
\end{remark}
\begin{definition}[Polarity Function]
	We define the function $d : \set{0,1} \rightarrow \set{+,-}$ with $d(0) = -$ and $d(1) = +$ as the \textbf{polarity function}.
\end{definition}
\begin{definition}[Destiny Star]
	Each star is indexed in binary notation denoted by $V_\alpha$ where $\alpha \in \set{0,1}^n$. Specifically, a binary string $\alpha = (a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n) \in \{0, 1\}^n$ uniquely defines a subset in the Cartesian product space:
	$$V_\alpha := \prod_{i=1}^{n} \Omega_{d(a_i)}$$
	In this formulation, $V_\alpha$ is defined as a \textbf{destiny star}.
\end{definition}
\begin{definition}[Phenomenal Image]
	For $\alpha \in \set{0,1}^n$, each element $v_\alpha \in V_\alpha \in V$ is called a \textbf{phenomenal image}.
\end{definition}
\begin{definition}[Star Space]
	The set of all destiny stars corresponding to resolution $n$ is defined as the \textbf{Star Space}, denoted by $V$. It is straightforward to see that $\card V = 2^n$.
\end{definition}
\begin{definition}[Semantic Function]
	Given $\alpha \in \set{0,1}^n$, for each $V_\alpha \in V$, we define $m : V_\alpha \to S$ as the \textbf{Semantic Function} of $V_\alpha$, where $s \in S$ is an interpretive proposition corresponding to $v_\alpha \in V_\alpha$.
\end{definition}
\begin{definition}[Domain Space]
	Let $\beta \in \{0,1\}^{\frac{n}{2}}$ with $n$ being the destiny resolution. We define the partition of the universal space $\Omega$ into $2^{\frac{n}{2}}$ disjoint subsets as the \textbf{Domain Space}, denoted by $\mathcal{C}$.

	Each subset $C_\beta \in \mathcal{C}$ represents a domain of life, defined by:
	$$C_\beta := \prod_{i=1}^{\frac{n}{2}} \Omega_{d(b_i)}$$

	The domain space $\mathcal{C} = \left\{ C_\beta \mid \beta \in \{0,1\}^{\frac{n}{2}} \right\}$ satisfies the partition axioms:
	\begin{enumerate}[label=\roman*)]
		\item $\bigcup_{\beta \in \{0,1\}^{\frac{n}{2}}} C_\beta = \Omega$
		\item $C_{\beta_1} \cap C_{\beta_2} = \emptyset \quad \forall \beta_1 \neq \beta_2$
	\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\chapter{Destiny Dynamics Model at Resolution $n=8$}
Returning to the general theoretical foundation established in Chapter 1, selecting a specific resolution plays a crucial role in balancing model granularity and computational efficiency. In this chapter, we investigate the Destiny Dynamics model at resolution $n = 8$.

Selecting $n = 8$ yields a symmetric binary structure that is informationally optimal:
\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{Star Space ($V$):} At $n = 8$, the cardinality of the star space reaches $\text{card } V = 2^8 = 256$ destiny stars. Each star $V_\alpha$ is encoded as an 8-bit binary string, representing a micro-interaction state of the universe.
	\item \textbf{Domain Space ($\mathcal{C}$):} At domain resolution $k = \frac{n}{2} = 4$, the human experience domain is partitioned into exactly $\text{card } \mathcal{C} = 2^4 = 16$ life domains. The 4-bit binary string length enables macro-classification of life contexts across an independent 4-dimensional coordinate system.
\end{itemize}

Furthermore, an 8-bit ($1\text{ byte}$) encoding scheme allows the Destiny Dynamics model to be directly integrated and processed on modern computer architectures, fully transforming traditional predictive discipline into a structured data processing algorithm.

This chapter focuses on formally defining the 16 life domains, structuring the 256 destiny stars, and constructing allocation mapping functions as well as interaction operators between Stars and Domains on a chart.

\section{Decoding the Domain Space}

At resolution $n=8$, the Domain Space $\mathcal{C}$ consists of exactly $2^4 = 16$ disjoint subsets. To construct the semantic function $m: C_\beta \rightarrow S$ naturally and rigorously, we model each binary code string $\beta = (b_1, b_2, b_3, b_4) \in \{0,1\}^4$ as a position within a 4-dimensional human domain coordinate system.

\subsection{The 4D Binary Human Coordinate System}

Each bit $b_i$ in string $\beta$ represents a binary dichotomy over the space of human experience:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
	\item \textbf{Bit $b_1$ -- Experience Space Dimension (Internal vs. External):}
	      \begin{itemize}
		      \item $b_1 = 0$ ($\Omega_-$): Internal, belonging to selfhood, spirit, latent foundations, or private life.
		      \item $b_1 = 1$ ($\Omega_+$): External, belonging to physical context, public environment, and outward manifestation.
	      \end{itemize}

	\item \textbf{Bit $b_2$ -- Interacting Subject Dimension (Self vs. Others):}
	      \begin{itemize}
		      \item $b_2 = 0$ ($\Omega_-$): Subjective/Self, subjective internal focus on the individual entity.
		      \item $b_2 = 1$ ($\Omega_+$): Objective/Others, external interactive relations with other entities.
	      \end{itemize}

	\item \textbf{Bit $b_3$ -- Origin Nature Dimension (Natural vs. Structured):}
	      \begin{itemize}
		      \item $b_3 = 0$ ($\Omega_-$): Natural, innate, lineage-based, organic emotion, or genetic heritage.
		      \item $b_3 = 1$ ($\Omega_+$): Structured, self-created, socialized, conventional, institutional, or accumulated achievement.
	      \end{itemize}

	\item \textbf{Bit $b_4$ -- State of Motion Dimension (Static vs. Dynamic):}
	      \begin{itemize}
		      \item $b_4 = 0$ ($\Omega_-$): Static, foundational, accumulated state, resources, or latent potential.
		      \item $b_4 = 1$ ($\Omega_+$): Dynamic, action-oriented, transformative, operational, or career/profession.
	      \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Structural Semantic Analysis of the Domain Space}

Rather than arbitrarily assigning static fixed names to each set $C_\beta$, the mathematical nature of Destiny Dynamics allows us to **construct the semantic function $m(C_\beta)$ via structural binary analysis** of the bit string $\beta = (b_1, b_2, b_3, b_4) \in \{0,1\}^4$.

Each string $\beta$ is not merely a label, but an **attribute vector** that governs the directional movement, interaction, and manifestation of all phenomena within that domain.

\subsubsection{Semantic Generation Mechanism from Attribute Vectors}

The semantic function $m: \mathcal{C} \rightarrow \mathcal{P}(S)$ (where $\mathcal{P}(S)$ is the power set of interpretive propositions) is constructed based on the synthesis of structural characteristics:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
	\item \textbf{Pure States and Yin/Yang Bias:}
	      \begin{itemize}
		      \item Code $\beta = (0,0,0,0)$ (\textbf{Pure Yin} State): Represents a fully receptive, absolute accumulating, internal, static, absorbing, and latent space. All phenomena characterized by selfhood, stillness, subconsciousness, or deep foundational elements converge into $C_{(0,0,0,0)}$.
		      \item Code $\beta = (1,1,1,1)$ (\textbf{Pure Yang} State): Represents a fully expressive, absolute manifesting, external, explosive, dynamic, and public space. All phenomena involving high-level social interaction, wide influence, or outward manifestation converge into $C_{(1,1,1,1)}$.
	      \end{itemize}

	\item \textbf{Antagonistic Structure and Opposition ($\oplus$):}
	      Two domains $C_\beta$ and $C_{\beta'}$ are defined as completely semantic opposites if $\beta' = \overline{\beta}$ (meaning $\beta \oplus \beta' = (1,1,1,1)$). The interaction between these two domains reflects the Yin--Yang equilibrium between internal latency and external exposure.

	\item \textbf{Semantic Completeness:}
	      Because $2^4 = 16$ binary strings cover all state combinations across 4 attribute axes, any event, situation, or entity in real-world experience can be mapped (projected) directly into its corresponding set $C_\beta$ based on its attribute vector.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{remark}[Dynamic Nature of the Semantic Function]
	Language interpretation $m(C_\beta)$ is not constrained by traditional destiny terminology (such as Wealth, Career, Spouse palaces), but expands into a multidimensional analytical tool: Any aspect of life—whether in ancient or modern societal contexts—is an element $\omega \in \Omega$ belonging strictly to one of the 16 structural states $C_\beta$.
\end{remark}
